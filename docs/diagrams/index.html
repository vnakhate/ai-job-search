Hosted half, local half, and the bridge

The drafter session fetches the posting as untrusted data, scores it with the gates and five dimensions, asks you, then writes both LaTeX documents with three-source grounding.

A fresh reviewer session researches the company and returns exact edits; the drafter applies them, compiles and measures the PDFs, checks the text layer, and records the tracker row.
